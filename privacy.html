Privacy Policy — Workspace MCP

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Workspace MCP is a private tool used only by Omer Alraee to manage his own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: read-only access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts and Google Sheets, for accounts owned by Omer Alraee only.

How data is used: data is read on request to help organise the owner's own email, calendar and files. Content may be processed by an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) for that purpose. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage: access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. The tool cannot send, change or delete anything in Google accounts.

Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
